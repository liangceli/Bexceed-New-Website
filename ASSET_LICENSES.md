# Asset provenance

## Brand and concept imagery

The Bexceed mark was supplied by the client. The white version is the approved contrast variant. Rights remain with its owner.

Hero poster, service photos, adviser poster and contact photo were carried forward from the user-approved homepage concept. The office clean plate and transparent benefit illustrations were prepared from that approved direction using image generation. These are illustrative visuals, not representations of actual Bexceed staff, clients or premises. Original design files and generation provenance remain in the local `design/` folder; web-optimised assets are in `public/assets/`.
